pastecmd

Privacy policy · last updated November 21, 2026

In short

pastecmd doesn't keep what you paste or send, can't read it, and doesn't know who you are. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.

What you send

Text and files are encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before they leave your device. The key is in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to a server. The relay passes the encrypted data to the other devices in your session as it arrives and never saves it. Filenames are encrypted too.

What the server keeps

For each session, the relay stores only its random ID and the device limit (2 or 8) while it's active. All of it is deleted when the session ends after 10 minutes with no activity.

Network data

pastecmd runs on Cloudflare. Like any website, its network sees your IP address and basic request details (such as your browser type) to deliver the page, protect it from abuse, and apply rate limits. pastecmd doesn't add to that data, sell it, or use it to identify you.

Stored in your browser

  • Whether you turned on "Allow Multiple Users" (local storage on the computer that starts sessions).
  • A random token that lets a tab reconnect to its own session after a reload (session storage, which is cleared when the tab closes).

Neither is sent anywhere for tracking, and you can clear both in your browser settings.

Third parties

The page loads one outside image: an integrity badge from codecanary.org that confirms the site's code matches what was published. No other outside scripts, fonts or trackers load. The site's Content-Security-Policy blocks them.

Children

pastecmd doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. The code, including the full security model, is public at github.com/HotStartLabs/pastecmd. Questions: security@pastecmd.com.
