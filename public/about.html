pastecmd

Copy & paste between your phone and computer. Nothing to install.

What it is

pastecmd is a free web tool for moving text, links, commands and files between your own devices. Open pastecmd.com on your computer, scan the QR code with your phone's camera, and anything you type or paste on one device shows up on the other right away. There's nothing to install and no account to make.

It's built for everyday chores: getting a link from your phone onto your laptop, sending a terminal command from a desktop to a phone, or moving a screenshot or PDF between devices without emailing it to yourself.

How it works

  • Your computer makes a random session and a secret key and puts both in the QR code. The key sits in the URL fragment (after the #), which browsers never send to a server.
  • Both devices connect to a small relay on Cloudflare's network. Everything you send is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM first, so the relay only ever sees ciphertext and can't read it.
  • Files up to 50 MB are split into encrypted chunks and sent through the same relay. Nothing is written to disk.
  • A session holds 2 devices by default (up to 8 if you allow it), and it ends after 10 minutes with no activity.

FAQ

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads, no accounts and no paid tier.
Do you store what I paste?
No. Text and files go from one device to the other through the relay and are never saved. The server can't decrypt them anyway. See the privacy policy.
What does it work on?
Any recent browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone or Android.
How much can I send?
About 700 KB of text, or files up to 50 MB each.
Can someone else join my session?
Only someone who has the link or sees the QR code. The device counter on the page shows how many devices are connected. If it's higher than you expect, start a new session.
Is the code public?
Yes. pastecmd is open source under the MIT license: github.com/HotStartLabs/pastecmd. The README describes the full security model.
Who makes it?
HotStart Labs, the makers of passburn.com.
How do I report a security problem?
Email security@pastecmd.com (see security.txt). For bugs and feature ideas, open an issue on GitHub.
